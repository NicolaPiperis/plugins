# Italy and the European Union

Where each kind of norm lives:

- **law**: Normattiva, EUR-Lex, the Gazzetta Ufficiale, and the guidance of the authority that applies it, such as the Agenzia delle Entrate, the Garante privacy, INPS or the Ispettorato del lavoro;
- **collective agreement**: the national agreement the customer applies;
- **standard**: the body that issues it, such as the OIC, the IFRS as endorsed by the EU, UNI or ISO;
- **practice**: sources that describe how the sector commonly works.
