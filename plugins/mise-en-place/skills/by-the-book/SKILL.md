## The point

The point is a piece of behaviour: what the product does, or what the user proposes it should do.

## The territory

The norm is the one of the territory the customer works in, as the user names it. When the conversation does not name it, ask once, before any research, offering as your recommendation the territory the product points to - its language, its currency: a territory guessed gives a whole answer under the wrong law. For Italy and the European Union, read [references/italy-eu.md](references/italy-eu.md): it says where each kind of norm lives there. For another territory, look for the same kinds in that country: its official gazette and legal database, the authorities that apply each law, the collective agreements of its sector, its standards bodies.

## The search

A norm already reported in this conversation is reused as it stands; search only what it does not cover.

For the rest, call the Skill tool with "mise-en-place:sourcing" and wait for it: the answer comes before the next step. Its request carries the point, what the conversation knows of the customer and its sector, the territory, the web as its source, where each kind of norm lives and the levels below, and the place for the file your caller names - with none, sourcing's own.

## The levels

Each claim carries its level:

- **obligation**: the law, or the collective agreement the customer applies. Say which of the two. It binds the customer.
- **standard**: a recognised reference, followed unless there is a reason not to.
- **practice**: how others commonly do it. A suggestion.

## The answer

Write it from the research file, as ASD-STE100 asks: short sentences, active voice, one piece of information per sentence, everyday words, the same word for the same thing throughout. Its length is what understanding needs.

For each norm that applies: what it asks, its level, and its source with the link and the date read. Then what the software does there, as far as the conversation already holds it. Where the conversation holds nothing on the software, say that the comparison stays open.

When no norm applies, say so, and name where the research searched.

**Done when** every norm that applies carries its level and its source, and the user has the path of every research file the answer stands on.
