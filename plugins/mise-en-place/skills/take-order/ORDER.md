# The designed order

The design goes into the ticket's `order.md`, below the request as it was made, in the sections its template, `config/templates/tickets/order.md`, gives a designed order. It is the ticket's first draft of its recipe, written from the functional side: the developer completes it in `grill-from-pantry`. Synthesize what the session has settled; a functional point it did not settle goes back to its branch.

Use the names the pantry fixes - the glossary, the vocabularies, the docs, the entries - throughout the order.

**The pantry is named only where it adds.** An entry that confirms a decision stays out of the order: it sends the reader back over a decision the session has already paid for. A pantry fact that overturns a decision sends it back to its branch before the order is written.

**The norm enters the order only beside a decision that goes against an obligation:** name the obligation, law or collective agreement, and its source.

Show the design whole and write it on the user's go. The request as it was made stays as it stands, above the design.

**Done when** the order holds every section of a designed order, and every decision of the session sits in it.
